The most important part of this process is something not listed: identifying your workflow’s goal. This is done by completing relevant background research, talking to stakeholders, and identifying what data you have available to you.

Sometimes goals are not able to come to fruition. This is normal, and requires you to rescope your goal to something that is doable within your time frame.
